# Creature Bible v1: Starters + First Enemy

**Formula:** 1-2 types + niche real animal + myth/history anchor + random object.
**Visual bar (Bloons TD6):** one-glance silhouette, thick outline, saturated flat-plus-gradient shading, exaggerated expressive faces, readable at ~64px on a phone.

## Type triangle (starter loop)
Ember > Verdant > Tide > Ember. Each creature's second type adds a twist (see "Hook").
Super-effective = 1.5x, resisted = 0.67x. Full 12-type chart is the next pass.

---

## 1. TICKINDLE (Ember / Forge): the striker
- **Animal:** Pallas's cat (manul), the flat-faced, wide-set-ears steppe cat.
- **Myth:** Hephaestus's golden automatons, the forge-god's self-moving servants.
- **Random:** Pocket watch.
- **Design:** Squat, fluffy cat with a cracked pocket-watch chest-plate glowing like a coal. Ember "second hand" tail ticks around. Palette: charcoal fur, orange glow, brass.
- **Role:** Single-target, high burst. Attack speed ramps the longer it hits the same target ("wind-up").
- **Hook (Forge):** Every 10th shot is a molten bolt that leaves a burn patch on the lane.
- **Evolution seeds:** Path A, Clockwork Pyre (rapid-fire). Path B, Anvilmanul (slow, armor-piercing).

## 2. DUGLOOM (Tide / Spirit): the controller
- **Animal:** Dugong. Sailors mistaking dugongs for mermaids is real history.
- **Myth:** Selkie/siren sea-lore.
- **Random:** Lighthouse lamp.
- **Design:** Round, gentle sea cow with a glass lantern on its head. Ghostly blue wisps trail its flippers. Palette: deep teal, pearl white, warm lamp-yellow.
- **Role:** Area slow and crowd control. Light beam sweeps the lane.
- **Hook (Spirit):** Slowed enemies reveal hidden/phasing foes, which counters stealth units later.
- **Evolution seeds:** Path A, Beaconmaid (wide slow aura). Path B, Wreckwarden (pulls enemies backward).

## 3. HUTZIN (Verdant / Venom): the damage-over-time
- **Animal:** Hoatzin. Chicks have wing claws and the bird smells, famously.
- **Myth:** Baba Yaga's hut on chicken legs.
- **Random:** Teapot.
- **Design:** Scruffy punk-crested bird with a mossy cottage-teapot on its back that steams spores. Palette: moss green, plum-purple venom, warm wood.
- **Role:** Poison stacks and lane-wide DoT. Slow attacker, strong late.
- **Hook (Venom):** Poison stacks spread to the next enemy in line when a poisoned foe dies.
- **Evolution seeds:** Path A, Brewmother (stronger stacks). Path B, Walking Cottage (becomes a tanky blocker that taunts).

---

## 4. SHUCKIT (Umbra / Gale): the first-level enemy
- **Animal:** Bat-eared fox.
- **Myth:** Black Shuck, the spectral dog of English folklore.
- **Random:** Broken umbrella.
- **Design:** Skinny, comically huge ears made of a torn black umbrella that catch wind. Glowing white eyes, harmless-looking. Palette: ink black, storm gray, pale yellow eyes.
- **Role:** Fast fodder, weak, comes in swarms. Teaches the player the lane and the type triangle.
- **Stats (draft):** HP 20, speed 1.2x, reward 5 coins. Leaks cost 1 life.
- **Hook:** Gale gives a small speed burst when a wave-mate dies, which rewards area damage over single-target.
