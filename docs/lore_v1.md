# Wellfall: World and Lore Bible v1

## Premise
Long ago the world drank from twelve **Wells**, deep springs of raw elemental power. Each Well fed one **Type** and the creatures bound to it. The Wells are now failing. As each one runs dry, the creatures it fed grow restless and drift toward the Wells that still flow. The last settlements cluster around those Wells, and every season the wild herds arrive in **waves**.

You are a **Wellkeeper**. You cannot, and would not, kill the herds. You raise **Companions** (creatures who chose to stand with you) and hold the lanes until each wave breaks and the herd turns back. When a map is cleared, a species from that region sees you hold the Well and **befriends** you: it joins your side as an Ally.

The tone is warm, odd and slightly eerie: a storybook where every creature is stitched from a real animal, a myth, and something mundane.

## The twelve Types (Wells)
Each type beats two others and is beaten by two others. Super-effective = 1.5x, resisted = 0.67x.

| Frost | The White Lantern | Verdant, Gale | Ember, Forge |
| Gale | The Hollow Wind | Venom, Forge | Volt, Frost |

Design rule: every type has an identity in *how it plays*, not just a damage tag. Ember burns, Forge builds, Tide controls, Spirit reveals, Venom spreads, Volt chains, Frost freezes, Stone crushes, Umbra hides, Gale hurries, Verdant grows, Aether bends.

## Creature formula
**1-2 types + a niche real animal + a historical figure, place, event or myth + a random everyday object.** Every roster entry below follows it.

## The maps (regions)
- **Mossgate Lane**: a meadow gate at the edge of the Green Hollow. Gentle, rain rolls in on later waves.
- **Frostrun Pass**: switchbacks in the snow below the White Lantern. Cold favours the Spirit; heavier herds.
- **Emberflats**: a zig-zag desert over the Kindling Deep. Heatwaves empower Ember and Forge; the hardest herds.

## Weather
Weather changes per wave and shifts the matchup: **Rain** boosts Tide and Volt (+20%) and dampens Ember and Forge (-15%). **Snow** boosts Spirit (+15%) and dampens Ember and Verdant. **Heatwave** boosts Ember and Forge (+20%) and dampens Tide. The HUD previews the next wave's weather so players can adapt before it starts.

## The Well Surge
The Wellkeeper's own power: once every 45 seconds, send a pulse through every lane. It stuns and damages every enemy on the map. It is the player's active tool for emergencies, and a tension against spending it early.

## Where the story goes next (open threads)
- Why are the Wells failing? Candidate answer: something is drinking from beneath them. The boss herds are not wild, they are being driven.
- Three of the twelve Wells have a region so far (Verdant, Frost, Ember). The other nine (Tide, Volt, Stone, Gale, Venom, Forge, Umbra, Aether, Spirit) mean nine more regions and nine more Ally species.
- Evolution lines for Allies, and a **Keeper's Hall** meta-hub where befriended creatures live and gift small permanent boons.
