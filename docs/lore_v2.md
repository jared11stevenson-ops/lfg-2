# Wellfall: World and Lore Bible v2 (10 levels, 20 creatures)

Supersedes `lore_v1.md` for the roster and levels. The type chart is unchanged (every type beats 2 and is beaten by 2; 1.5x / 0.67x).

## Premise
Twelve **Wells** fed the world's elements and the creatures bound to them. They are failing. Herds drift toward the Wells that still flow, in **waves**. You are a **Wellkeeper**: you raise **Companions** and hold the lanes until each herd turns back. Clear a level and a species from that region sees you hold the Well and **befriends** you, joining as an Ally. A perfect run (3 stars) befriends a bonus creature.

## Art direction
Hand-drawn 2D cartoon in the spirit of late-night surreal animation, picture-book whimsy and midnight paintings: flat saturated colours, thick dark outlines, noodle limbs, mismatched bug eyes, wonky proportions, Seussian curls and stripes, with a Pokemon-style hint of cute proportions, types and evolution. Levels are painted at night: brushy indigo and teal grounds, glowing lanes, moon pools, rune stones and fireflies in each biome's accent colour. Every creature is an **original** character built from the formula: **1-2 types + a niche real animal + a myth/history anchor + an everyday object**, and must have a readable silhouette, a face with an attitude, and an animation personality (grumpy, sleepy, manic, smug...).

## Replayability systems
- **Procedural waves:** each wave's budget grows and species are drawn by weight from the level roster (seeded per run), so no two runs match. A species gets an introduction wave. Boss on wave 10.
- **Difficulty tiers:** Easy, Normal, Hard, Nightmare (HP, speed, lives, coin, sell refund).
- **Ten mutators** (pick up to 3): Swift Herd, Thick Hides, Lean Times, Swarming, Fragile Wells, Fog of Well, Elite Herd, Relentless, No Surge, Heavy Air. Each raises the score multiplier.
- **Stars:** 1 clear, 2 with half your lives left, 3 also needs Hard+ or 2+ mutators. 3 stars befriends a bonus creature.
- **Daily Challenge:** one seeded level, Hard, two random mutators, the same for everyone that day.
- **Endless mode** after a clear, with a boss every 5 waves.
- **Build depth:** 20 defenders x 2 upgrade paths x 4 tiers (only one path past tier 2), targeting modes, rune stones, weather and type matchups, support auras (attack speed, range), economy defenders, status combos (Wet + Volt, chill stacks, curses), the Well Surge ability.
