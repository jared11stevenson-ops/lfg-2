# Rules notes, pass 2 (for the lead: what exists, exactly what to wire)
Everything below lives in `rules/`, `data/`, `ui/standing_*.gd`, `ui/skill_tree_*.gd`, `tools/sim_*`. Nothing in `main/` or `world/` was touched.

## 1. Win and score
- **Total Conquest is the only win.** `Economy.check_victory` fires `victory{kind:"total_conquest", faction:int, player:bool, holdings, day, faction_name}` once, when exactly one faction is alive AND it owns every holding. `state.won == "total_conquest"`; the match does not end (Endless is the player's choice). Dominion (60%) and Crowned (12 weeks) are gone (`Calendar.CROWNED_WEEKS`, `Economy.DOMINION_FRACTION` removed).
- `MetaHooks`: title `lfg:title/total_conquest`, granted only when `player == true`. `lfg:title/dominion|crowned` no longer exist.
- **TODO for you:** `ui/game_end_screen.gd` still has `"dominion"`/`"crowned"` strings and `tools/art_capture_pass1.gd` calls `show_victory({"kind":"dominion"})`; switch both to `total_conquest` (info keys: `day`, `class_label`, `holdings`, `total_holdings`, plus `Standing.hud_model` prosperity for the end screen).
- **A leader's death eliminates ANY faction** (`Combat.kill`): player faction = `match_over` (permadeath, as before); AI/bot faction = `faction_eliminated`, its holdings become neutral, the garrison routs (`garrison/3`) and its walls and towers fall. `faction_eliminated` also runs `check_victory`.
- **Prosperity** (non-ending thriving score): `state.factions[fid]["prosperity"] = {score, output, reserves, holdings, ration_days, thriving}`, recomputed each dawn; `Standing.prosperity(state, fid)` computes it live. Output accrues from harvests, production buildings and AI farming (`Standing.note_output`).
- An AI faction that starves for 28 days collapses (`famine_collapse`, its leader perishes). The player keeps the old Starved Out rule.

## 2. Standing, ranks, deeds (data/ranks.json, rules/standing.gd)
- Standing = holdings by tier + capped ration reserve + **Purge deeds** that decay linearly over 21 days. Thresholds `[0,6,14,26,42,64]` scale by `sqrt(14 / live factions)` clamped to 0.8-2.5. Recomputed at every dawn (`Standing.recompute`), also exposes `Standing.score/threshold/rank_for_score`.
- Rank effects are production/access/logistics only (growth %, harvest yield steps, plot slots, carry cap for capped classes, garrison cap, supply range, merchant price %). No combat stat reads rank (a test asserts it).
- Access gates: crops (`rank` in crops.json: saltroot R1, glowfruit_prime R3, moonbean R4, purgebloom R4, crownwheat R5), recipes (`rank`), buildings (`rank`: barracks/granary/wall_stone R1, watchtower/cookhouse/steam sawmill/shrine R2, beacon/greenhouse/forge R3). Commands answer `rank_required`.
- **Thin crown:** rations under 3 days or any holding with stability < 40 -> `eff_rank = rank - 1` for buffs (access still uses `rank`).
- **The Mark:** the top-standing faction with rank >= 2 (`state.sim_acc["mark"]`, -1 = none). Striking the Marked (kill leader, capture, steal, ...) multiplies the deed's standing and skill points by 1.5; AI target score x1.35; a Marked player faces bigger waves; an AI Mark loses garrison at Purge start (`mark_pressure`).
- **Deeds** are derived from the event stream by `Standing.observe` (called by `Rules.tick` per step and by `Rules.apply`) and only count in PURGE_ACTIVE, with per-Purge caps (data). Kinds: `kill_leader capture kill_raider ransom steal defend sabotage kill_wild heal broker scout escort`. Every deed pays skill points to the faction leader.
- **Anti-snowball:** `dawn_upkeep` charges coin upkeep growing as `n^1.4/3.5` beyond 2 holdings (tax income `tier` coin/holding/day), per-holding stability (distance from the capital, extra holdings, hunger, unpaid upkeep), revolt at stability <= 12 (never the capital), unstable holdings produce less, and an **idle bleed** drains single-holding factions with no deed for 14 days. Peacetime spoilage grows with the week (granary halves it).
- **Purge Levy** after RESOLUTION starts (`Sim.settle` -> `Standing.charge_levy`): per holding rations+coin by tier, growing 12%/week (cap x1.6), cut by fighting deeds (up to 80%), a Shrine (25%) and tree nodes. Unpaid: stability -12 on every holding and a garrison deserts. `Standing.levy_due(state, fid)` previews it.

## 3. Purge pressure and ways in
- **Purgebloom** (`purge_only` crop, rank 4): plant >= 2 hexes from the holding; it never grows in peace and never withers; `water` it during PURGE_ACTIVE to tend it (`tended`); each tended Purge (at `Sim.settle` -> `Farming.purge_growth`) grows one stage; ripe after 2. **Ashmarrow** drops from wave beasts and raider parties and feeds the rank-4/5 seed and ration recipes.
- Escalation: wave size `2 + week` (cap 14) around the player (plus `2 + week/2` more while the player is Marked); AI dispatch slots, party size and party count grow with the weeks (`AIFactions.max_dispatch/party_cap/max_parties`).
- Scarcity: AI production `x(1 - 2%/week)` (floor 0.5), harvest yield loses steps from week 3 on, spoilage.
- New ordinary commands (all Purge-only, none needs another player; pacifist-legal except sabotage):
  - `scout {holding}` within 3 hexes of a rival holding, once per holding per Purge -> `scouted`.
  - `escort {holding, qty>=3}` stand at an own holding >= 5 hexes from the capital -> rations move into its store -> `escorted`.
  - `truce {faction}` (WARNING or PURGE_ACTIVE) within 3 hexes of that AI faction's holding; pays 15 coin into its store; mutual truce 45 s x tscale -> `truce_signed`. Quill's ability also writes `factions[x].truces`.
  - `ransom {target}` adjacent enemy leader at <= 40% HP: up to 30 coin moves to you, 45 s mutual truce, once per victim per week -> `ransomed`.
  - `sabotage {holding}` adjacent rival: steal-chance roll, burns up to 5 rations (destroyed) -> `sabotaged` / `sabotage_failed`.
  - `unlock_skill {node}`: see 4. There is **no respec**: `respec` is an unknown command.
- Errors you may see: `rank_required needs_outdoors purge_only already_scouted no_rival not_outlying already_escorted already_truced not_beaten already_ransomed nothing_to_burn node_locked not_enough_points already_taken unknown_node`.

## 4. Skill trees (data/skill_trees.json, rules/skills.gd)
- 14 trees x 15 nodes, branches `economy / purge / hybrid`, each branch has a cost-1 root; `h3` needs an economy and a purge node; 2-3 costed drawback nodes per tree (cost >= 3, a real penalty). Effects are small deltas with per-key caps (`caps` in the file); max_hp/atk/def/max_energy apply once at unlock, the rest live in `actor["skill_fx"]` and reach the rules through `Combat.class_mod` (so `yield_bonus`, `steal_bonus`, `build_cost_mult`, ... just work) and `Skills.tune_ability` (cooldown, energy, duration, power).
- **Points come only from Purge deeds** (`Skills.grant`, refuses outside PURGE_ACTIVE). Actor fields: `sp` (unspent), `sp_total`, `nodes` (taken ids), `skill_fx`. AI/bot leaders spend at once (`auto_buy`, branch order by persona).

## 5. Class-aware AI (data/ai_kits.json, rules/ai_leaders.gd, rules/ai_factions.gd)
- `Setup.build` deals the 13 non-player classes to the 12 AI leaders (seeded; opt `ai_classes:false` keeps them classless). Leaders have real class stats (`Setup.set_leader_class`).
- Dawn: peace routines through the real abilities (mine, forage, bless; abstract tonic/gadgets/hunt/trade/yield/grove/ration bonuses). Purge start: non-raider personas and diplomats pay tribute for a truce (`truce_signed`); Dune stashes a real cache.
- Raids/defence: brawler/warlord/skirmisher/thief classes march with capture/steal parties, hostile parties harass any hostile leader next to them, leaders trade blows and cast their kit with real arguments (`AILeaders.siege_cast`: rage, plague cloud, dash with target and free hex, pickpocket target, ram holding, swarm at foe, ...). Brittle leaders (effective HP < 70) stay home. Leaders within 6 hexes reinforce a besieged holding (`defend_call`/`recall`). Pacifist classes never raid. Passive personas run `scout` and `supply` parties that earn deeds; late-game/fat-garrison factions turn ambitious.
- Bounded CPU: nothing here runs per tick except sieges; measured in `tests/rules/test_ai_classes.gd` and the sim report.

# PASS 3 (quests, points of interest, resource tiers, buildings, sim fixes)
Everything below lives in `rules/` (`quests.gd`, `pois.gd`, small edits elsewhere), `data/` (`quests/*.json`, `pois.json`, items/recipes/buildings/ranks/ai_kits), `world/` (`poi_models.gd`, `poi_layer.gd`, 17 building models, `WorldStage` wiring), `ui/` (`quest_panel.gd`, `poi_panel.gd`, HUD tracker, toolbar button, map markers). **Nothing in `main/` or `project.godot` was touched.** Owner rules held: every quest, recipe, building and node is completable by a self-sufficient solo player (tests/rules/test_quests.gd proves it from content data: start kit, home store, crops, enemy loot, POI loot, class abilities, craftable outputs; no merchant and no other player is a source); no friendship/reputation/trade value exists (a test scans rules and data for them).

## 1. Quest engine (rules/quests.gd, data/quests/index.json + one file per line)
- **State lives on the actor** (`state.actors[id]`): `quests{quest_id:{st:"active"|"done", step, prog, ready, day, rewarded}}`, `unlocks[]` (ids like `lfg:unlock/draught`), `quest_fx{}` (signature ability upgrades, added on top of the class mods by `Combat.class_mod`/`Skills.tune_ability`, not subject to skill-tree caps), `titles[]`, `pois_seen[]`. A quest with no record is **locked** (prerequisite quest not done, or another class's line) or **available**. Old saves have none of these fields; every read tolerates that and the first accept creates them.
- **Commands** (all in `Rules.COMMAND_TYPES`): `accept_quest {quest}`, `abandon_quest {quest}`, `turn_in {quest}`. Errors: `unknown_quest quest_locked already_active already_done not_implemented too_many_quests` (max 6 active), `not_active nothing_to_turn_in not_near_own_holding not_at_place insufficient_items`. `turn_in` on a *ready* quest (all steps done) claims the reward at an own holding (within 3 hexes); on a quest whose current step is `deliver` it consumes the items at the place (`to:"holding"` or `to:"poi:<poi type>"`, i.e. next to such a POI) and advances (a final deliver completes at once). Abandoning resets progress, never pays, and the quest becomes available again. A done quest can be neither re-accepted nor abandoned; rewards are granted exactly once.
- **Events**: `quest_accepted{actor,quest,title}`, `quest_step{actor,quest,step,text,steps,next}`, `quest_ready{actor,quest,title}`, `quest_done{actor,quest,title,reward,line,signature}`, `quest_abandoned{actor,quest}`.
- **Step kinds** (`Quests.STEP_KINDS`): `gather{item,qty}` (possession, not consumed), `build{building}` (state), `visit_poi{poi,qty}` (state: discovered points of that type), `craft{recipe,qty}`, `harvest{crop|any,qty}`, `plant{crop|any,qty}`, `ability{ability,qty}`, `claim_poi{poi|any,qty}`, `defeat{enemy|any,qty}`, `purge_deed{deeds[],qty}`, `survive_purge{qty}` (event-driven: only counted while that step is current), `deliver{item,qty,to}` (consumed by `turn_in`). State-based steps cascade (already satisfied steps complete on accept). Optional `theme:"lfg:class/x"` marks a step that nods to another class's theme (convergence); it never needs that class or any other player.
- **Rewards** (`reward{items, unlock, ability_upgrade{key:delta}, title, text}`): items go to the pack, overflow to the nearest own holding's store (never lost). `unlock` is appended to `actor.unlocks`; recipes/buildings with `requires_unlock` answer `locked_recipe` / `locked_building` until then.
- **Observer**: `Quests.observe(state, events, from, actor_hint)` runs inside `Rules.apply` and `Sim.advance` (cheap when no events). `Quests.journal(state, actor)` -> `{active, available, locked, done, soon, titles, unlocks}` rows `{id,title,line,chapter,intro,state,ready,implemented,signature,steps[{index,text,kind,done,current,progress,qty,theme,item}],step_index,reward,unlock,class,requires}`; `Quests.tracker(state, actor)` -> `{quest,title,text,ready}`; `Quests.toast_for(event)` -> `{title,body,kind}`.

## 5. Pass-2 sim findings fixed (numbers: docs/SIM_REPORT_pass3.md)

## 1. Why classes were unfair (diagnosis from the per-class death logs)
The pass-3 sim had no death log; `tools/sim_match.gd` now records, for every leader death, `{day, tag, source kind, killer class, where (home/own/foreign/open), hp fraction before the killing blow, holdings, garrison}`, plus per-class ability use (`kit`), a fortnightly holdings series and, at a famine collapse, the larder of the previous dawn (the state at the collapse is already wiped). `tools/sim_deaths.py <dir>` prints the per-class table. What the logs showed (pass-3 code, 24 seeds):
- **Leader deaths were a kit lottery.** Only Vex/Quill/Mira/Dune could slip out of a lost holding (`escape.flee`); Pip (17 deaths), Nyx (15), Sable (15), Bram (14), Tavi (12) stood and died at hp 0.11-0.5 at their home door.
- **Passive personas were the default victims.** Hoarder/trader/turtle factions sit on one holding all match; raiders take their last roof as soon as they can. Persona (random per class and seed) decided more than class: hoarder 81% eliminated, raider 63%. The harness now deals personas as a Latin square over class and seed (`--random-personas` restores the old behaviour), so persona noise cancels out of the class table.
- **Famine collapsed ~13% of all factions**, nearly all of them lone holders: the scarcity curve (-2%/week, floor 50%) leaves a one-holding larder at about 2 rations a day, the pass-2 idle bleed took up to 4 more, and garrisons kept being recruited on an empty larder.
- **No path to land for thieves/diplomats**: Vex (steal 3), Quill, Dune, Sable, Halo never took a village unless their persona was raider.
- **Total Conquest stalled in the endgame** (a 38-holding empire with 46 coin in total cannot recruit: pacify and levy eat the coin; the last survivor, usually a pacifist Halo behind Warding Aura plus last stand, was never finished).

## 2. Rule changes (all numbers in data/)
- **Mercy** (`ranks.json mercy`): before the hunt a raid never takes a rival's last roof (`last_roof` 0), two roofs are x0.4 targets, three x0.75, a holder above 1.8x the average of holders is x1.3. Used by `AIFactions._try`; `AIFactions.mercy(n, avg)`. `until_week` 99 = for the whole match; the hunt ignores it. Elimination now comes from the hunt, famine-free decapitation and revolts, not from every passive faction being eaten at week 16.
- **Settlers**: every non-pacifist faction may take **unowned** villages from week 1 whatever its persona (`_plan`: `settler` = not a raider and capture bias < 2; `_try(..., neutral_only)`). Settlers look for open land before they look for pockets to pick.
- **Conquerors**: `AIFactions.hunting(state, fid)` is also true for a faction that holds `hunt.conquer_at` (14) holdings or `conquer_share` (34%) of the map: it plays the last-rival hunt for itself (x3 parties, farther sight, muster from the realm, weakest-survivor targeting, rations levy) while everyone else keeps living normally. `hunt.late_week` (99 here) is the optional Reckoning: a week after which everyone hunts whatever the head count. `max_parties/party_cap/max_dispatch` take an optional `fid`.
- **Ration levy** (`hunt.levy_per_day` 1, `levy_rations` 2): a hunting realm that is fed (>= 3 days of food) turns granary rations into troops where coin has run out. Without it conquerors with 30+ holdings sat on garrisons of 2.
- **Ration discipline**: no recruiting on an empty larder (`furlough.recruit_days`), a governor under one day of food sends 25% of every garrison home (`_furlough`, event `furlough`), any hungry governor (not only traders) buys rations at the nearest market, every AI holding makes at least `subsist.per_holding` (3) rations a day (kitchen gardens), and the idle bleed only takes the *surplus* rations (it nudges, it no longer starves). Famine collapses: 43-50 per 24 matches -> 0.
- **Leader escape for everyone, by kit**: `escape.evac` (data/ai_kits.json, 0.3 Bram/Rook .. 0.8 Vex) is the chance that a wounded leader (< 40% hp) slips away, and at the start of a siege that cannot be held (`attacker_power * 0.85 > defender_power`, `AILeaders.on_siege_begin`) the chance that the leader leaves before the blows. Falling back goes to another own holding not under siege, else into open ground until dawn (`AILeaders.fall_back`). A leader behind a strong garrison stays; decapitation strikes (party weaker than the walls) are unchanged. `furlough.evac_scale` scales all of it.
- **Home ground** (`classes.json mods.home_def`, applied in `Conquest.defender_power`): Vex 1.45, Dune 1.5, Sable 1.4, Pip 1.3, Tavi 1.3, Quill 1.25, Mira 1.2, Nyx 1.2, Bram 1.1, Forge 1.1, Oren 0.9, Rook 0.85 (Rook also upkeep 0.9, was 0.7: Rally Banner already doubles as +40% defence). Dune's gadget stock is a defence in itself (+1.5 per gadget, cap 10).
- **Bot triggers for the two unused abilities**: Quill raises the real **Truce Flag** the moment a siege of its holding begins (`cast: "truce"`; the raiders then ignore the flagged leader, `_harass`); Sable casts the real **Track** at every dawn (`peace: "hunt"`; earns the scout deed when game is in range). Pip and Sable only join theft parties (`join_only: ["steal"]`), they no longer die marching on walls.
- **Halo's hamlets** (`mods.settle` 6, `ClassKits._settle`): Bless on an unowned village in reach makes it a hamlet of the Warden's, once per calendar week, up to six holdings; the pacifist's only way to grow. Event `holding_settled`. The bot walks to the nearest unowned village within 10 hexes on its even days (`AILeaders._pilgrimage`, through the real `Abilities.use`).
- Rook's capture bias 3 -> 2, Oren home_def 0.9 (see report for the measured effect).

## 3. Questlines (data/quests/{vex,quill,nyx,rook,dune,halo}.json)
`stubs.json` is gone; every class has a three-chapter, eight-step original line ending in a signature upgrade (`quest_fx`): Vex *Light Fingers / Borrowed Time / The Long Quiet* (+10% steal chance), Quill *A Fair Hand / Ink and Amber / The Flag and the Ledger* (Truce Flag +10s), Nyx *Under the Caps / Grove Tending / The Quiet Choir* (outside groves 85% instead of 75%), Rook *Raise the Banner / Muster the Road / Hold the Line* (Rally Banner cooldown -20%), Dune *Odds and Ends / Buried Things / The Better Latch* (takes 1.10x instead of 1.25x damage), Halo *First Steps on the Road / Alms and Blessings / The Long Vigil* (Warding Aura +10s). Same validation as the other eight (`tests/rules/test_quests.gd`: 14 lines, 6-8 steps, signature ending, known step kinds, every item/building/recipe obtainable solo, no trade step, POI types placed in generated worlds).

## 4. Known gaps
Class fairness is measured on bots; Oren and Rook still win more than their share, Vex/Dune hold the least land; the per-class numbers move with the 40-seed sample (see report). Persona is still the biggest single driver of any faction's outcome. The sim does not play quests.
